\# 📖 Overview



\*\*DomVerse\*\* is a centralized Hostel Management System developed to automate and simplify hostel administration.



Instead of relying on manual registers, paperwork, and physical approvals, DomVerse provides a secure digital platform where students can submit requests, wardens can manage hostel activities, and administrators can monitor everything from a single dashboard.



The platform improves communication, reduces paperwork, increases operational efficiency, and enhances transparency between students and hostel authorities.

\# 🎯 Problem Statement



Traditional hostel management relies heavily on manual paperwork, physical approvals, and disconnected communication methods, leading to:



\* Delayed approvals

\* Data redundancy

\* Record maintenance issues

\* Poor communication

\* Lack of transparency



DomVerse addresses these issues by providing a centralized digital management platform.
